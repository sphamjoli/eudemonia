Eudemonia model

Tokenise the attestation, not the raw data.

Employer policy is configured on-chain; compliance records stay off-chain. Settlement only executes after proof verification.

Infrastructure features

Eudemonia is infrastructure, not a wallet dApp.

The engine is a set of reusable rails: PolicyRegistry, IssuanceRegistry, PaymentRegistry, TokenisationEngine, SP1 verification, and ConfidentialSettlement.

  • 1
    Role-based governance: admin controls participants and operational permissions.
  • 2
    Policy-bound execution: each request is constrained by active policy and privacy rules.
  • 3
    Proof-gated settlement: execution proceeds only when `(parametersHash, policyHash)` verification passes.
  • 4
    Configurable privacy: no privacy, destination private, amount private, or full private.
  • 5
    Institutional auditability: request, policy, attestation, proof, and receipt remain independently traceable.
Live walkthrough
3 commands · 60 seconds

Employer configures policy, then each Alice payment is privately executable and publicly auditable.

In one pass: employer creates policy, issuer submits Alice's request, attestor signs, worker proves, chain settles. On-chain: commitments + proof + receipt. Off-chain: sensitive compliance records.

Commands
make demo-setup → Employer sets Alice's policy: required checks, approvers, threshold, and allowed privacy modes.
make demo-request demo-attest demo-status → Issuer submits Alice's payment request, approver signs, worker proves, and settlement executes on-chain.
make demo-audit → Auditor retrieves Alice's full trace: policy → request → attestation → proof → settlement receipt.
Three commands. One policy-controlled payment flow.
